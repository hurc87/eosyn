Privacy policy

This page explains what information we handle on this website and in our app, Pharos, and why.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

  • Our website has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Pharos works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts and no servers, and it doesn’t collect anything about you.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.

We’re the “data controller” for the information described here. That means we decide how it’s used, and we’re responsible for looking after it.

If you have any questions, email us at support@eosyn.co.uk.

Our website

eosyn.co.uk is a simple website. It sets no cookies, has no analytics and doesn’t track you. Two other companies help deliver it to you.

GitHub, who host the site

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may log your IP address so it can keep its service secure. We don’t see these logs. You can read how GitHub handles this in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Google Fonts, for our typeface

Our website uses the Montserrat typeface, which your browser downloads from Google Fonts. To send you the font, Google receives your IP address and basic details about your browser. We don’t receive any of this. You can read how Google handles it in the Google Privacy Policy.

GitHub and Google are based in the United States, so this information may be processed there. We rely on our “legitimate interests” as the legal basis for this, because we need the site to load, look right and stay secure.

If you email us

If you email us, we’ll use your email address and anything you tell us only to reply to you and help with your question. We won’t add you to a mailing list or pass your details to anyone else.

Your email is stored with our email provider, IONOS. We keep it while we’re dealing with your question. We delete it within two years, unless we still need it, for example because a support issue is still open. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us.

Pharos

Pharos works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts and no servers, and we don’t run any system that the app sends data to. That means we have nothing about you to store, share or lose.

Camera

Pharos asks for camera access for two reasons:

  • To use the torch. On phones, the torch is part of the camera, so the app needs camera access to switch the light on and off. No photos or video are taken.
  • To read Morse code. When you point your camera at a flashing light, Pharos looks at the brightness of the image, live, on your phone, to work out the message. The images are never saved and never leave your phone.

You can say no to camera access. Pharos will then flash the screen instead of the torch. You can change your mind at any time in your phone’s settings.

Torch

The torch is used only to flash signals. No data is involved.

Your messages

Any message you type for Pharos to flash in Morse stays on your phone. It isn’t sent to us or anyone else.

What Pharos doesn’t have

Pharos has no analytics, no ads, no crash-reporting tools and no tracking.

App Store and Google Play

You download Pharos from Apple’s App Store or Google Play, and Apple and Google handle that under their own privacy policies. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your phone’s settings, Apple or Google may give us anonymous statistics, such as download numbers or crash reports. These don’t identify you. You can turn this sharing off in your phone’s settings.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • ask what information we hold about you, and get a copy
  • ask us to correct anything that’s wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to how we use it, or ask us to limit it
  • ask us to send your information to you or someone else in a format that’s easy to reuse

In practice, the only information we’re likely to hold about you is an email you’ve sent us. To use any of these rights, email support@eosyn.co.uk. We’ll reply within one month, and there’s no charge.

Making a complaint

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Children

Pharos and this website don’t collect personal information from anyone, children included. If a child has emailed us and a parent or guardian would like that email deleted, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we’ll update this page and the “last updated” date at the top. If Pharos ever starts collecting anything, we’ll update this policy before that change reaches the app, and we’ll say so in the app’s release notes.
