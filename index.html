We’re a small studio making apps, games and websites.

Eosyn was started by two founders who want to build things that are well made, genuinely useful, and free of tricks and dark patterns.

Our name comes from two Greek words: eos, meaning dawn, and syn, meaning together. A new dawn, together.

Our first app

Pharos

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

Pharos turns your phone’s torch into an emergency signal. It flashes SOS, sends your own messages in Morse code, and can read Morse back using the camera.

It’s named after the Lighthouse of Alexandria, a light that guided people to safety.
