What Pharos does

Pharos turns your phone’s torch into a signal light. It flashes the SOS distress signal in Morse code.

Pharos is free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create.

Please read this: Pharos is a signalling aid

Pharos can help you attract attention, but it cannot guarantee that anyone will see your signal or come to help.

  • Always contact the emergency services first if you can. In the UK, call 999 or 112.
  • Whether your signal is seen depends on things we can’t control, such as distance, weather, daylight, obstacles, and whether anyone is looking.
  • Flashing the torch uses battery. Keep your phone charged, and think about saving battery for calls if you’re in trouble.
  • How the torch behaves depends on your phone. Some phones may stop the torch when the screen locks or the app is closed. Test Pharos on your phone before you need it.
  • Your phone may get warm if the torch runs for a long time.

Using Pharos safely

  • Don’t shine the torch directly into anyone’s eyes.
  • Flashing lights can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Take care using Pharos around others, and don’t use it if flashing lights affect you.
  • Only signal SOS when you genuinely need help. False distress signals can waste rescuers’ time.

Your privacy
